## Goals

- General strength development across all major patterns (vertical/horizontal push/pull, hinge, squat)
- Upper body strength priority > lower body strength
- Muscle growth in weak areas
- Shoulder health and balance
- General health (cardiovascular + metabolic)
- Maintain bodyweight, improve composition

## Weak Areas and Matched Tools

| Weakness | Tools |
|---|---|
| Bench — first 1/3 off chest | Paused bench (F), wide-grip bench (W), pec flyes |
| Squat — out of the hole | Paused back squat (Sa), front squat (Th), BSS |
| Deadlift — off the floor | Deficit deadlift (Sa), paused deadlift (Th) |
| OHP — first half | Dead-stop OHP (all OHP sessions), hypertrophy DB press (W) |

## Structure

- **6 sessions/week**, alternating Upper/Lower
- **Upper days:** Monday (U1 — heavy), Wednesday (U2 — light), Friday (U3 — moderate)
- **Lower days:** Tuesday (L1 — heavy), Thursday (L2 — light), Saturday (L3 — moderate)
- **Sunday:** Rest
- **Heavy/Light/Moderate** is defined by movement stress and volume, not %1RM
- **Deload:** every 4 weeks (halve accessory sets, mains at ~60%)
- **Progression rule:** to be formalized after first deload; planned approach — *if top-set RPE ≤ 7, add minimum increment next session; if RPE ≥ 9 or reps missed, hold; two holds → deload that lift 10%*

## Notation

`WEIGHT × SETS × REPS` (e.g., `135×2×5` = 135 lb for 2 sets of 5)

## General Health Layer

- **Steps:** 7,000–10,000/day floor
- **Mobility:** 10 min × 2–3 days/week (t-spine, hip flexors, ankles, shoulder CARs)
- **Bloodwork:** annual baseline (lipids, fasting glucose, HbA1c, vitamin D)

## Tracking During Block 1 (4 weeks)

- Top-set RPE on each main lift, every session
- Weekly bodyweight average (not daily)
- Subjective recovery: sleep quality, session energy, joint feel (shoulders, wrists, low back)

## Future Considerations (Post-Deload)

- Formalize progression rule (RPE-based, above)
- Confirm Front Squat TM, establish SLDL/paused DL/BSS working weights
- Farmer's carry load progression (distance → time → added belt load)
- Good mornings — progress toward meaningful hinge stimulus (~40–50% of DL TM)
- Ab rollouts — progress to 3 × 8 clean reps, then deficit or banded
- Optional: sandbag conditioning day (replacing, not adding — medium shell, ~80 lb starting fill)

## Non-Negotiables

- Wrist wraps on all heavy pressing
- One full rest day per week (Sunday)
- Deload every 4 weeks
- Don't solo-test true 1RMs in a squat stand
